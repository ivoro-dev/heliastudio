# Brief

## The client
Héléna Clinckart, founder of Helia Studio. She works alone. She writes LinkedIn content for other people (ghostwriting) and rebuilds their LinkedIn profiles, in English and French.

## The offer (two things only)
1. **Done-for-you LinkedIn content:** finding topics, writing posts that sound like the client, designing visuals, scheduling.
2. **LinkedIn profile optimization:** headline, About section, banner, and making it clear what the client does and who they help.

Her old page and LinkedIn also list graphic design, email marketing, lead generation and so on. They are NOT on this page. The page leads with the LinkedIn specialism. A narrow, clear offer is better for trust and for search.

## The audience
Defined by situation, not by title or industry: people who sell their own expertise and whose next client checks their LinkedIn before deciding to get in touch. Founders, consultants, coaches, advisors, independent professionals.
Job seekers are a secondary audience. They are served only by the one-time Profile Blueprint, never by the headline or hero.

## The one action
Book a free 30-minute discovery call: https://cal.com/helenaclinckart/discovery-call
Secondary: send her a DM on LinkedIn (URL [TODO]).
Every button on the page leads to one of these two. No other conversion paths.

## The differentiator
The client works directly with her. She writes, designs, schedules and publishes everything herself. No middlemen, no account managers. This must be visible in the design (her face, her voice, first-person copy), not only stated.

## What changes from the old page
The old HTML page is a content source only. It used common template patterns: a marquee ticker, glow and grid backgrounds, a fake "Clarity 31/100" score, card grids, three competing CTAs in the pricing. We keep its best ideas (the before/after headline, the direct-work promise, fixed prices, the profile review before the call) and drop its look.

## Trust: honest proof
She has one client so far and few testimonials. The page earns trust through:
- her own results, shown in public
- her real work shown as real artifacts
- a clear, specific process
- her visible presence
- transparent prices

Rules:
- Never invent testimonials, logos, client names, numbers, reviews or scarcity.
- A client logo or quote appears only with that client's written permission. Default: none.
- Every figure shows its source and date.
- Hypothetical examples are labelled "Illustrative example".

## Voice
**English:** plain, direct, warm. First person ("I"). Short sentences. Specific over abstract. No hype, no exclamation marks.
Banned words: elevate, unlock, empower, seamless, supercharge, game-changing, transform your, take your X to the next level, journey, leverage.

**French:** vouvoiement. Natural French written or reviewed by Héléna, not machine translation. Gender-neutral phrasing wherever possible. Keep the LinkedIn terms people actually use (post or publication, carrousel).

Copy is plain text styled with CSS. Never use Unicode "bold" or "italic" characters (screen readers and search engines handle them badly).
